## First, identify where you purchased

Subscriptions through the ChatGPT website, Apple App Store, Google Play, and a third-party top-up service have different billing and support processes. Check the receipt or order record to identify the merchant before troubleshooting.

## If an official paid plan is missing

1. Confirm that you are signed in to the same ChatGPT account used for the purchase, including the email address and sign-in method.
2. On the device showing the Free plan, sign out and sign back in to the account used to subscribe.
3. Check the receipt to identify whether the subscription was purchased on chatgpt.com, through Apple, or through Google Play.
4. If the plan is still missing, follow the OpenAI Help Center instructions to contact OpenAI Support.

OpenAI recommends checking the ChatGPT account used for the purchase when a paid plan does not appear. If you subscribed through more than one platform, check each platform's subscription record separately.

## If this is a third-party top-up order

Keep your order number, order time, and checkout receipt. Do not post a password or full payment-card details publicly. Check the account status using the product page instructions, then contact the service with your order details if activation is still pending.

This site currently lists support hours as 9:00–22:00 China Standard Time and says common orders usually take 5–30 minutes. It also states that a top-up that cannot be completed is fully refunded. Those are this site's service terms, not OpenAI's refund policy. The external checkout page is currently in Chinese.

## Avoid duplicate subscriptions or payments

If you see more than one charge, check subscriptions on chatgpt.com, Apple App Store, and Google Play, including the account used on each platform. Cancellations and refunds must be handled through the platform that collected the payment; eligibility depends on that platform's policy.
