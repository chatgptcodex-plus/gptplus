## Start with the work you need to do

Before choosing a plan, list your common tasks: everyday questions, file analysis, image generation, deep research, or intensive coding and long work sessions. Then check which options your account can currently purchase and what limits apply. Plan names alone do not tell you whether an option is available for your account, country, or purchase channel.

## How Go, Plus, and Pro are positioned

- **Go** is a lower-cost option for people who want more access to everyday ChatGPT features than Free. The available tools and limits depend on the current plan shown in your account.
- **Plus** is an individual plan with expanded access to ChatGPT tools and advanced features. Its models, features, and limits can change.
- **Pro** is intended for higher-intensity use and generally offers higher usage access. OpenAI currently describes several monthly Pro tiers; check the official plan page and your account for the available tiers, prices, and limits.

As of this article's update, OpenAI's Help Center describes multiple monthly Pro tiers. Names, prices, and usage limits may change. Prices shown by a third-party service are not OpenAI's official subscription prices.

## Check these four things before choosing

1. **Which tools do you need?** Confirm that the current official plan includes the models and features you rely on.
2. **Can your account subscribe?** Region, account status, and purchase channel can affect availability.
3. **Where will the subscription be billed?** Web, Apple App Store, and Google Play subscriptions are managed separately. Subscribing through multiple channels can lead to duplicate charges.
4. **Is the price official or from a service provider?** OpenAI's official plan price and a third-party service's CNY quote are different prices. Check both before paying.

## What do the 5x and 20x labels on this site mean?

The “5x” and “20x” labels on this site's GPT page describe the options listed by this service. They are not OpenAI plan names. Before ordering, confirm account eligibility, the activation method, and the current quote. The external checkout page is currently in Chinese.
