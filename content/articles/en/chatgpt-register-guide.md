## Before you sign up

First, check [the countries and regions supported by OpenAI](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7947663-chatgpt-supported-countries) and confirm that ChatGPT is available where you are. Follow OpenAI's Terms of Use and the instructions shown during sign-up.

Use an email address that you can access long term. Do not share your verification code or account password with anyone.

## Sign-up steps

1. **Open the ChatGPT website:** Go to [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com/) and choose the sign-up option.
2. **Enter your email and create a password:** You can also use another sign-in method offered on the page.
3. **Verify your email:** Check your inbox and spam folder, then follow the verification email.
4. **Complete your profile:** If prompted, enter your name, birthday, or other requested details.
5. **Complete any additional verification:** Phone or other checks depend on the current sign-up flow. If you cannot complete them, contact official OpenAI support. Do not try to bypass regional or account restrictions.
6. **Sign in again:** Confirm that you can access the account, then check whether the plan you want is available for it.

## Common questions

### What if the verification email does not arrive?

Check your spam folder, confirm that the email address is correct, and use the page to request another message. If it still does not arrive, try another supported sign-in method or contact your email provider.

### Does creating an account include a paid plan?

No. Sign-up creates an account; it does not activate a paid plan. Features, limits, and availability may change. Check the [official ChatGPT pricing page](https://openai.com/chatgpt/pricing/) and the plan shown in your account.

### What should I check before upgrading?

Make sure you are signed in to the correct account, then check regional availability, account status, and plan eligibility. For this site's service method, required account information, and price, read the relevant product page and external checkout details.
