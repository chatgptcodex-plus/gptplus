## 1. Confirm the account you want to use

Check the email address and sign-in method shown in ChatGPT. If you use multiple email addresses, Apple sign-in, or Google sign-in, make sure the current session is the account you intend to upgrade. Do not send a verification code or password to support.

## 2. Check regional availability and account status

ChatGPT availability and plan options can vary by region, account status, and current official policy. Check [the countries and regions supported by OpenAI](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7947663-chatgpt-supported-countries) and follow the instructions on the official page. Do not try to bypass regional or account restrictions.

## 3. Confirm the billing channel and plan

OpenAI subscriptions may be managed through the web, Apple App Store, or Google Play. Billing and cancellation steps differ by channel. If you already have an active subscription through one channel, check before purchasing through another. Read the [plan comparison guide](chatgpt-plans-guide.html), then choose an option available in your account.

## 4. Separate official plan prices from this site's service quote

OpenAI's website lists official subscriptions. This site lists its own products and quotes; they are not the same price. Features, account eligibility, payment methods, and third-party service terms may also differ. Review the product page and external checkout before paying. The checkout page is currently in Chinese.

## 5. Share only the information the listing requests

This site states that most options do not require a password. Follow the information requirements on the specific product page. If you are ordering a preconfigured account, confirm what will be delivered and how to secure it after the first sign-in. Contact support before ordering if a requirement is unclear.
